A checkout that lets anyone, anywhere, pay in their own money

Extending Afriex Collect into a hosted, multi-currency, multi-channel checkout across EMEA, APAC and LATAM — with saved payment methods, Stripe Link–style.

My role
Senior Product Manager
Type
0→1 feature
Surface
Hosted checkout
Deliverables
PRD + prototype

The problem

Afriex Collect let businesses generate a payment link and collect funds without building a checkout — but it forced a single currency and channel. That's a poor fit for merchants selling across borders, where every customer wants to pay the way they already pay locally: a Nigerian buyer by bank transfer to a virtual account, a Kenyan via M-Pesa, a Filipino via GCash, a Brazilian via Pix.

The result was lost conversions and FX friction, or expensive bespoke integrations per market. There was no single hosted page that adapted currency and channel to whoever was paying, while still guaranteeing the merchant a predictable settlement amount.

How might we let a payer pay in any currency and channel they prefer, while the merchant always receives exactly what they expect?

My role

I owned this feature end to end as Senior Product Manager: framing the problem, defining scope and requirements, mapping the payer and merchant flows, and producing the PRD and interactive prototype used to align engineering and design. I worked closely with treasury on FX/rate-lock mechanics and with partnerships on channel coverage per corridor.

Approach

  1. Framed the core insightThe merchant and the payer want different things. The merchant wants a predictable settlement amount; the payer wants to pay in their own currency and channel. The product's job is to reconcile the two, with Afriex absorbing the FX in between.
  2. Designed for stepped disclosureRather than a wall of options, the payer moves through one decision at a time: see the amount → choose currency → see only the channels valid for that currency → pay. This avoids dead ends where a chosen currency has no viable channel.
  3. Made FX transparent and safeEach currency shows the converted amount and the market rate up front. Selecting a channel locks the rate for a 15-minute window, so the payer always knows the final amount before committing.
  4. Reduced repeat friction with Afriex LinkModelled on Stripe Link: payers can save their details and preferred method for one-tap checkout on future payments — recognised across any Afriex merchant.
  5. Specified the unglamorous edgesRate-lock expiry, under/overpayment on transfers, partner outages, duplicate payments, shared-device recognition — the cases that decide whether money actually moves.

The flow

The hosted checkout is mobile-first and follows the happy path below, with a returning-payer shortcut layered on top:

1. Amount → 2. Choose currency (with live rate) → 3. Choose channel (only valid ones shown, rate locks) → 4. Your details (save with Afriex Link) → 5. Pay (channel-specific instructions) → 6. Confirmation.

Returning payers are recognised by Afriex Link and land directly on their saved method with the amount pre-converted — a single "Pay in 1 tap" action, with the option to switch currency or channel.
