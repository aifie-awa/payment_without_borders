Processor Health & Failover — System Architecture

From live transaction telemetry to an automated, ops-authorised switch onto a funded backup processor — monitored down to each MNO wallet and bank.

Telemetry
Transaction events
Every attempt tagged with processor, service (country × type × method) and destination (MNO wallet or bank).
Stream / collector
Events aggregated continuously into success/failure counts per dimension.
Health engine
Success-rate calc
Rolling success rate per processor, and drill-down per MNO / bank, on an hourly window.
Trend store
Hourly history retained to power the live chart and spot degradation early.
Thresholds & alerting
Alert threshold
Below it → notify the ops team to review (degraded).
Failover threshold
A lower bar → trigger the failover evaluation (critical).
Failover orchestration
Backup lookup
Is a backup processor configured for this exact service?
Funding check
Is the backup adequately funded to absorb the volume?
Proposal to ops
If yes → propose a switch for authorisation; if underfunded / none → escalate for manual action.
Routing
Authorise / override
Ops authorises the switch, overrides to stay, or switches manually at any time.
Reroute
On authorisation, traffic for that service routes through the backup; the switch and its author are logged.
Failover decision sequence
1
Rate < failover threshold
Service breaches the lower bar — enter critical.
2
Backup configured?
No → escalate for manual intervention.
3
Backup funded?
No → block auto-switch, alert ops to fund or act manually.
4
Propose switch
Funded → present authorise / keep-current to ops.
5
Execute & log
On authorise, reroute to backup; recovery monitored.

Monitoring hierarchy & thresholds

Healthy — above the alert threshold. Normal routing.
Degraded — below alert, above failover. Team notified to review.
Critical — below failover. Failover evaluation runs.
Failed over — now routing via a funded backup after authorisation.

Drill-down: Processor → service (country × type × method) → destination (MNO wallet / bank), e.g. Kenya bank payout → UBA Kenya, Ecobank Kenya.

Design principles

  • Two thresholds, two responses. One to tell a human to look; a lower one to act. They are not the same bar.
  • Automate the detection, keep a human on the switch. The system proposes; ops authorises or overrides — with a manual switch always available.
  • Never fail over to a processor that can't take it. The funding check is a hard gate before any proposal.
  • Drill-down is the diagnosis. A processor looks "80%" because one bank is failing — the destination view shows which.
Primary objective: catch a failing processor, MNO or bank in real time and reroute to a funded backup — fast, with ops in control.
