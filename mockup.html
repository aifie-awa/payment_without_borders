A Afriex Collect Prototype · Multi-currency checkout
Interactive — click through the flow

A clickable prototype for the extended Afriex Collect feature: a hosted checkout page where a payer is shown an amount, chooses the currency they want to pay in (converted at Afriex market rates), then picks a supported channel — virtual account, mobile money, or digital wallet — across EMEA, APAC and LATAM. Payers can save their preferred method for one-tap reuse, Stripe Link–style.
