A variation, not a new product

This reuses everything from the core Collect checkout — hosted link, payer picks currency & channel, Afriex market rates, predictable settlement to the recipient. Only the configuration and presentation change.

What this scenario adds

The deltas that make "occasions / group gifting" its own use case:

👥
Many payers → one link
A running total and contributor count, not one invoice for one payer.
🎚️
Preset + custom amounts
Suggested gift tiers with a free-entry option, versus a fixed amount.
🧑
Individual recipient (P2P)
A person, not a KYB'd business — different limits & compliance posture.
💌
Branded, emotional page
Couple's names, photo, note & messages — not a utilitarian invoice.

Why it's cross-border by default

The guests were scattered by construction — coursemates from Mauritius now back in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and beyond. Every one of them pays in their own currency; the couple in Nairobi receives one predictable settlement.
