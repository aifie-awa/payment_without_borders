A clickable prototype of the AI rate-negotiation agent for a NGN → USDC trade. A business client negotiates the rate in chat; the agent interprets intent but every price comes from the deterministic engine. Once agreed, the client can lock the rate or proceed straight to execution: pay NGN into a virtual account, funds are confirmed, the client provides a USDC wallet, and the on-chain transaction hash is returned. What the client experiences

The end-to-end trade
The client negotiates a rate, then chooses to lock it or proceed. On proceed they get a NGN virtual account to fund; once payment is confirmed they enter a USDC wallet address, and the agent returns the on-chain transaction hash — the whole trade in one conversation.
