How to read this

Click a service to drill into its MNOs / banks and chart it. Press Simulate incident to drive the Kenya bank-payout service down: it will cross the alert threshold (amber, review), then the failover threshold (red), at which point the system checks for a funded backup processor and proposes a switch you can authorise or override. You can also switch any service manually.
